Safety Guidelines:

  • All participants must wear appropriate safety gear at all times.
  • Participants must follow all instructions from the staff and instructors.
  • Participants must not engage in activities that may pose a risk to their safety or the safety of others.
  • Participants must report any injury immediately to the staff.
  • Participants must respect the environment and wildlife during the activity.
  • Participants must maintain a safe distance from other participants at all times.
